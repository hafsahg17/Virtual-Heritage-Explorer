A Journey Into The Past

The Glorious Vijayanagara Empire

Hampi, located in Karnataka, was once the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire. From the 14th to the 16th century, it was a major centre of trade, culture, and architecture. Its magnificent temples, royal buildings, and stone monuments reveal the greatness of this ancient kingdom.

Historic Capital

Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire and an important centre of South Indian culture.

Stone Chariot

The famous granite chariot at Vijaya Vittala Temple is dedicated to Garuda, the sacred vehicle of Lord Vishnu.

UNESCO Heritage

Hampi was recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1986 for its outstanding historical importance.
