A Royal Legacy

History of Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace, also known as Amba Vilas Palace, is one of India's celebrated royal residences. The present palace was completed in 1912 and is closely associated with the Wadiyar royal family. Its architecture combines Indo-Saracenic and traditional Indian design influences. Grand halls, colourful glasswork, decorated interiors, and elegant domes make the palace remarkable.

Royal Residence
