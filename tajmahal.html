The Story of the Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal was commissioned by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Construction began in 1632 and continued for several years. The monument is famous for its white marble architecture, symmetrical gardens, beautiful calligraphy, and detailed stone inlay work. It stands beside the Yamuna River in Agra and represents a remarkable achievement of Mughal architecture.

Historical Period

Built during the Mughal Empire in the 17th century under Emperor Shah Jahan.

Architecture

A masterpiece of Mughal architecture combining Persian, Islamic, and Indian artistic influences.

UNESCO Heritage

The Taj Mahal was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1983.
